Direct Smartphone Email Attachment Workflow:

When you tap "Share GPS File", your phone's native share menu (Android / iOS) will launch. Choose Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook, or Messaging to automatically create an email draft with your live recorded .gpx session directly attached!

How do I attach the file manually on my phone if Web Share isn't supported?
  1. Tap "Download" or "Save .GPX" above to save the file into your phone's Downloads folder or Files App.
  2. Open your mobile email app (Gmail, Apple Mail, Samsung Email).
  3. Create a new email and tap the paperclip / attachment icon.
  4. Browse your smartphone file picker, select the downloaded sup-session-....gpx file, and hit send!
